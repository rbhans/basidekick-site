.04 Atlas specimen section / exploration

Four ways to make the specimen feel collected, indexed, and alive.

The current home section already has useful data. These options preserve the BASidekick dark instrument-panel feel while giving the Atlas specimen more visual identity.

Specimen cue Real specimen systems lean on unique IDs, labels, taxonomy, location, and supporting data. The Atlas card should feel like an indexed record, not just a promo card.
Dashboard cue The section should use the card as a navigation and compact information hub, with only the most relevant data visible first.
Dark UI cue Keep depth through dark grays, ruled lines, contrast, and negative space. Avoid turning it into a heavy black box full of tiny text.
Option 1 / Specimen tray

Best fit if the home page should still feel calm and editorial.

A larger specimen surface gives the point name room to breathe. The right side stays practical: sheet number, taxonomy, Haystack, Brick, aliases, and controls.
